How it fits into your week

Before

Record as usual

Zoom, Google Meet, a phone voice memo, or your laptop mic in person. Quill never joins your calls.

After the call

Drop the file into Quill

It turns the speech into text on your laptop, then reads it the way a good assistant would.

Two minutes later

Approve what it drafted

A list of promises, a note on who needs attention, and ready emails. Nothing is sent until you say so.

The same loop, for different kinds of work

Freelancers
Tracks deliverables and invoices, and notices a client who keeps delaying a decision.
Consultants
Tracks data requests and scope changes, and keeps a steady client on a light touch.
Coaches and tutors
Tracks practice plans, and catches a student who is about to skip a few weeks.
